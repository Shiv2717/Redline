Draft. Redline

a copy-editor's desk for text — reword it, condense it, or check how it reads

The paraphraser and summarizer run on built-in word lists and scoring, not a trained model — good for a quick pass, not a substitute for your own edit. The read-check score is a rough heuristic (sentence rhythm, word repetition, stock phrases), not a certified AI detector, and this isn't a plagiarism checker — that needs a live index of the web to compare against. If the goal is getting past a required-original-work check, that's worth thinking twice about either way.
